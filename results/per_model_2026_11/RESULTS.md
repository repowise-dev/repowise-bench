## A5 effect

A5 changed one regex (LEV-02 `half_life`: `\bhalf_life\s*\(` to `\bhalf_life\b`) and regraded both
arms of every model. It moved three runs from wrong to correct: Haiku 4.5 repowise 67 to 69 of 72,
Opus 4.8 bare 46 to 47 of 48. Every other cell is unchanged. `\bhalf_life\b` does not match inside
`default_half_life` (the underscore is a word character), so the other LEV-02 item is not satisfied
by it. Per-row before/after table below.

## Medians and sensitivity (not pre-registered, reported for completeness)

Median per-question change in output tokens: Opus 5.5 -10.3%, Sonnet 5.5 -10.0%, Opus 4.8 +1.4%,
Sonnet 4.6 -51.4%, Haiku 4.5 -6.4%. Opus 4.8's mean saving is carried mostly by FOR-07 (bare
7,205 and 5,795 output tokens, repowise 2,079 and 1,790); its interval spans zero and it is leaner
on 12 of 24 questions. Total tokens (input, cache
reads and writes, output) were recorded, not pre-registered as a headline metric; the difference is
within noise on every model, every 95% interval spans zero (table above). The repowise arm loads its
tool schemas in the first turn (always-load), which is where any difference comes from.

Misses by question: MISSES.md (haiku-4-5 and sonnet-4-6). Repowise-arm misses concentrate on
LEV-10 (Rust path-qualified calls `crate::index::open_ro(...)` not resolved as call sites; the tools
flagged `complete:false` and the agents answered anyway).
